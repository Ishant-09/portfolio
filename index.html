Argus

An experimental robotics concept exploring how an overhead camera could track fiducial markers and coordinate small mobile robots.

Python Computer Vision AprilTags ESP32 Microcontrollers
EXPLORE LAB NOTES
The Concept

Centralized optical tracking eliminates the need for expensive onboard sensors on individual miniature wheeled robots.

Proposed Approach

Mounting an overhead camera running AprilTag detection in Python to calculate each robot's 2D position and orientation, then sending wireless motor adjustments over ESP-NOW or MQTT.

What I'm Learning

Fiducial marker detection, perspective transformation calibration, and wireless telemetry to ESP32 microcontrollers.
