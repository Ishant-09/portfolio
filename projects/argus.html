PROJECT 03 // ROBOTICS & OPTICAL TRACKING

Argus

An architectural investigation into centralized overhead computer vision for coordinating miniature swarming mobile robots.

Problem & Motivation

In multi-robot research, equipping each miniature robot with lidar, stereo cameras, and inertial measurement units (IMUs) quickly drives up hardware cost, battery weight, and onboard computing requirements.

Argus investigates an alternative paradigm: centralized global perception. By mounting a single stationary camera above a defined arena, a host computer tracks high-contrast fiducial markers (AprilTags) on top of each robot, calculating their global coordinates and heading vectors with sub-centimeter accuracy.

System Topology

The planned system consists of three interconnected layers:

  • Overhead Vision Pipeline: A top-down camera capturing the arena. Software runs AprilTag detection to identify marker IDs, 2D position (X, Y), and orientation angle (θ).
  • Central Motion Coordinator: A Python supervisory process that computes path trajectories, obstacle avoidance vectors, and differential drive motor speeds.
  • Low-Power Robot Nodes: Small differential-drive chassis powered by ESP32 microcontrollers, receiving lightweight motor PWM packets over ESP-NOW or local Wi-Fi UDP sockets.

Exploration Roadmap & Research Goals

1. Perspective Transformation: Calibrating the camera matrix to eliminate lens barrel distortion and skew when mapping pixel coordinates to millimeter ground distances.

2. Low-Latency Wireless Control: Testing transmission intervals using ESP-NOW (a peer-to-peer protocol developed by Espressif) to achieve sub-10ms packet delivery without router overhead.

3. Hardware Assembly: Evaluating micro gearmotors, motor drivers (L298N vs. DRV8833), and 3.7V LiPo power regulation.

Current Status

Argus is currently in the conceptual design and component planning stage. No physical robots have been finalized yet; the exploration is focused on simulating the tracking mathematics and assembling the required bill of materials.
